How to swing
AimDrag across the course, or open the map and tap a target. The white ring is where a pure, full swing lands with no wind.
Tempo · left thumbTouch anywhere on the left and slide up for the backswing. The scale shows the carry for your club. Slide back down past where you started to strike. The downswing must take under 0.35 s for full power; slower costs distance. Backswing three times as long as the downswing gives the full smash factor; anywhere from 2:1 to 4:1 still strikes well. Putts want 2:1.
Strike · right thumbHold your thumb just below-right of the ball on the dial: the red dot sits up-left of your thumb so it never hides. It wobbles, wilder the faster you swing. Keep it on the sweet spot. With no thumb on the dial the dot slips loose round the edge of the face and the strike is always a mishit. Toe hooks, heel slices. High on the face spins less and flies further, low spins more.
FlagstickThe flag stays in for every shot but a putt. A firm ball running at the stick can't hop the hole: square on it drops in, a glancing hit ricochets away. Only a ball right on the edge of the cup can lip in or out.
RulesWater costs a penalty stroke and a drop. White stakes are out of bounds: one stroke and replay. Max score is par + 5.
KeyboardHold Space to swing back, release, press again to strike. Rest the mouse low on the right side to hold and steer the dot. Arrows aim and change clubs.
